About

Connecting physical understanding with data-driven intelligence.

My academic work has developed across Vietnam, South Korea and Japan, with a continuing focus on water-related hazards, hydrological prediction and the use of modern data methods to strengthen scientific understanding and decision support.

I am particularly interested in research problems where observations are incomplete, spatial variability is strong, and uncertainty matters — including rainfall reconstruction, floodplain representation, hydrological forecasting, remote sensing and the integration of machine learning with domain knowledge.

Research philosophyAI should complement — not replace — hydrological reasoning, physical modelling and scientific validation. My work therefore emphasizes performance, uncertainty, robustness and practical interpretability.

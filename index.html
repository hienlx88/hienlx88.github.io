About

Research shaped by water, data, and decisions.

My academic work has developed across Vietnam, South Korea, and Japan, with a continuing interest in how observations, physically based models, remote sensing, and artificial intelligence can be combined to improve hydrological understanding and water-related decision support.

I am particularly interested in research questions where data limitations and model uncertainty matter: flood-prone river basins, spatial rainfall estimation, floodplain representation, hydrological forecasting, and the transfer of modern data-science methods into operational water-resources applications.

About

Connecting physical understanding with data-driven intelligence.

My academic path spans Vietnam, South Korea and Japan, with a continuing focus on hydrological prediction, water-related hazards and uncertainty-aware modelling.

I am especially interested in research problems where observations are incomplete, spatial variability is strong and uncertainty matters: rainfall reconstruction, floodplain representation, streamflow forecasting, flood inundation, remote sensing and machine learning for water science.

Current focusFlood modelling, rainfall information, uncertainty quantification, remote sensing and AI-assisted water-resources research.

Research

Research pillars

Four connected themes define my current academic profile.

01 · HYDROLOGY

Hydrological Forecasting & Data-Driven Modelling

Streamflow, flood and water-level forecasting using deep-learning and hybrid frameworks, with emphasis on reliability and uncertainty.

02 · HYDRAULICS

Flood Inundation & Hydro-Hydraulic Modelling

Rainfall–runoff–inundation modelling, floodplain sensitivity, terrain uncertainty and robustness of simulated flood risk.

03 · EARTH OBSERVATION

Rainfall Estimation & Remote Sensing

Radar–gauge integration, satellite precipitation correction and remote-sensing approaches for flood and hydrological analysis.

04 · AI

AI for Water Science

Machine learning, deep learning, explainability, uncertainty and integration of data-driven methods with domain knowledge.

Research practice

Research tips & knowledge sharing

Short practical notes derived from water-science research practice.

Validate beyond one metric

Use bias, RMSE/MAE, peak timing and event-wise generalization alongside NSE or R².

Treat DEM as uncertainty

Terrain data can fundamentally change flood pathways, depths and inundation extent.

Keep the full research pipeline

Version data, preprocessing, model settings and evaluation protocols for reproducibility.

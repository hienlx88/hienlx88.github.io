Academic Bio

From classical hydraulics to AI for water science.

Xuan-Hien Le is a Lecturer at the Faculty of Water Resources Engineering, Thuyloi University. He received his Ph.D. from Kyungpook National University, South Korea, in 2020, continued there as a postdoctoral researcher, and served as an Associate Professor at the Research and Development Initiative, Chuo University, Japan, from 2024 to 2026.

His research lies at the intersection of hydrology, hydraulics, disaster-risk science, remote sensing and data science. A recurring theme is the reliability of information: whether rainfall inputs are trustworthy, how terrain uncertainty propagates into flood maps, how confident an AI prediction should be, and whether a model is fast enough to support practical decisions.

This has naturally led toward hybrid approaches that combine physical models, observations and machine learning, as well as surrogate modelling for computationally expensive hydro-hydraulic simulations.

In one sentenceAccuracy matters, but understanding why a model works, when it may fail and how fast it can respond often matters just as much.

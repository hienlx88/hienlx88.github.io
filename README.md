---

## Research Overview

![Research overview](assets/research-overview-hero-vi.png)

My current and continuing research interests include:

- **Hydrology & flood forecasting**  
  Streamflow, water-level and flood prediction; rainfall–runoff modelling; deep learning for forecasting.

- **Rainfall & meteorological observations**  
  Satellite rainfall, weather radar, radar–gauge merging, bias correction and spatial rainfall analysis.

- **Flood & hydro-hydraulic modelling**  
  Rainfall–runoff–inundation modelling, DEM uncertainty, flood extent and flood depth.

- **Remote sensing for water and floods**  
  Sentinel-1 SAR, optical remote sensing, flood monitoring and water-related Earth observation.

- **Hydraulics & hydraulic structures**  
  Applied hydraulics, flow through hydraulic structures, modelling and hydraulic performance assessment.

- **AI, hybrid & surrogate modelling**  
  Physics–data integration, uncertainty, explainability, hybrid modelling and reduced-order / surrogate models.

- **Landslide & geospatial machine learning**  
  Landslide susceptibility mapping, feature selection, explainable AI and uncertainty analysis.
